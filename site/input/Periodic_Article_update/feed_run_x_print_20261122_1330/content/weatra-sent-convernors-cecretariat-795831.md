This is to bring to your kind notice that Hon'ble Governor has conceptualized and initiated a Faculty Development Programme for the capacity building of the faculties to cope up with the new challenges of the curriculum, pedagogy & assessment in the light of National Education Policy (NEP-2020). The FDP shall be conducted in the Universities and Constituent Colleges in Bihar.

Prof. B.K. Tripathi, Founder, Director of Inter University Centre for Teachers, Education (IUCTE), UGC, MOE, Govt. of India has been appointed as Advisor to Hon'ble Governor for Faculty Excellence & Academic Develonment (FEAD). In order to monitor smooth conduct of FDP effectively, Prof. B. K. Tripathi will be visiting at least one FDP in each district.

It is desired that the programme being conducted in a Commissionerate headquarter district should be in the knowledge of the Commissioner to contribute towards the quality of the programme and to attend the programme as Chief Guest in the Inaugural/Valedictory session.
