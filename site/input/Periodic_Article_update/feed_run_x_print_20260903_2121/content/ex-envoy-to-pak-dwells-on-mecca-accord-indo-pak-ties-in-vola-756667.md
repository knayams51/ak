# Ex-envoy to Pak dwells on Mecca Accord, Indo-Pak ties in volatile situation

**Patna** — Former Indian diplomat 'TCA Raghavan, who served as the Indian High Commissioner to Pakistan from 2013 to 2015, on Monday said that in the growing and lingering conflicts in the world have shown that there could not be military solutions to political problems.

He was speaking at the Istedition of Lecture Series, Pataliputa Pragya Pravah, at the Lok Bhawan on "Mecca Accord, Pakistan and the future of Indo-Pak Relations". Referring ( the RussianUk e war and US-Iran conflict, Raghavan said the repercussions are for all to sce despite the involvement of two big powers against not so big powers.

Emphasising on a clinical and not a sentimental appreciation of the turbulent situation, he underlined the need for a longterm approach on the part of India to manage its South Asian neighbourhood. "Foreign policy is nota zero sum game and itis perfectly possible for different powers to have multi-pronged engagement in order to serve their self-interest.

Besides, the very nature of warfare is changing and the factor of asymmetry between unequal powers is getting reduced," he added.
