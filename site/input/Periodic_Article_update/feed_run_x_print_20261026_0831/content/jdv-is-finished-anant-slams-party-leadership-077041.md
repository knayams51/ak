# JD(V) is finished’: Anant Slams party leadership

**Patna** — A day after expulsion, Janata Dal (United), Mokama MLA Anant Singh remained defiant and said that some leaders of the party are taking advantage of "weakened party supremo Nitish Kumar", adding that the party was heading towards political doom.

Singh, along with his wife Neelam Devi, a former legislator, was on Saturday evicted from the party for six years over "antiparty" activities as he continued to support a rival candidate against his own party's nominee in the legislative council elections. "The party belonged to Nitish ji. Now that he is no longer there (in an active role), what is left of the party? Who among those in the leadership, the ones who expelled me, has actually contributed anything?" he told reporters. "Now, they will be reduced to 10-12 seats... Meanwhile, other outcomes of Singh's expulsion are also being speculated. Some political pundits feel that he might float his own party and contest the bypoll from Mokama or push his. son in the electoral fray should his disqualification from the House is done.

RJD MP from Buxar, Sudhakar Singh, said that nothing would go wrong for Anant Singh, as he is a powerful leader and would stay in politics. "He is not with us. There are rumours of his joining Jan Suraaj. He has people's support. But yes, the future of JD(U) certainly looks bleak," he added. The RJD, on the other, hand posted a remark, saying that traitors are receiving the punishment for their deeds", in an oblique reference to Anant Singh's wife Neelima Singh who had switched sides to extend support to the Nitish government during the 2024 confidence vote.

Some political observers saw Singh's expulsion as the JD(U) taking rebellion and indiscipline in the part seriously ahead of the Council poll result on October 27.
