# Dy CM downplays feud’ within JD(U)

**Patna** — arunhs @hndustantenes.com

Bihar deputy chief minister Vijay Choudhary on Tuesday made light of the ongoing infighting within the JD(U) between party MLC Neeraj Kumar and party MLA Anant Singh over the Legislative Council election for Patna (graduate) seat, saying the matter would be resolved in due course.

Singh, the party MLA and a strongman from Mokama, on Monday vowed to "defeat" his party's MLC Neeraj Kumar in upcoming legislative council polls by supporting and canvassing for Jan Suraaj Party candidate Anuj Singh.

Singh admitted that he was settling "personal" scores with Neeraj Kumar, who is seeking re-election from the Patna Graduates constituency for a fourth consecutive term.

"The matter is in the party's consideration and the issue will be resolved well before time." he added, while addressing media persons at the JD(U) office. Choudhary also did not delve into Union minister and LJP-RV chief Chirag Paswan's remark that "he felt ashamed of being part of the government due to poor law and order situation", and cited the government's prompt response as the mark of "good governance",

"No crime is an ideal situation, But the true test of the government and administration begins only after a crime takes place and action taken against the offenders vouch for it. What Chirag said is nothing unnatural, as some incidents have taken place of late and swift action has been taken against the anti-soCials. His party is part of the government." he added.
