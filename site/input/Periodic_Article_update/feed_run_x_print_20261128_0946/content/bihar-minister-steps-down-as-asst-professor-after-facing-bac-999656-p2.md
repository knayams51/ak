# Bihar minister steps down as asst professor after facing backlash

**Patna** — PATNA; Bihar's food and consumer protection department minister Ashok Choudhary has submitted a resignation from an assistant professor position in the political science department at Patna's AN College. a senior official said on Friday. The move came after the minister faced backlash over his association with the "office of profit", which escalated to the Lok Bhavan, which referred it to the Advocate General.

Patliputra University (PPU) registrar Professor Abu Bakar confirmed the development, but did not mention the date from when the resignation would be effective. "After being selected by the Bihar State University Service Commission, | joined and applied for leave immediately. However, there has been no decision on my leave application due to which | have decided to resign," a university official quoted the resignation letter, adding that it would be placed before the University Syndicate.
