# Caught in ‘office of profit’ row, Bihar min resigns as asst prof

**Patna** — Bihar's food and consumer protection minister Ashok Choudhary has finally resigned from the post of assistant professors in the department of political science, AN College, Patna, registrar of the Patliputra University (PPU) Prof Abu Bakar said.

The minister was under constant pressure to quit his teaching job as it purportedly violated the Artiche 191(1\(a) of the Constitution of India disqualifies individuals from serving as members of a State Legislative Assembly or Council if they hold an "office of profie" under the central or state governments, unless that office is specifically exempted by state law.
