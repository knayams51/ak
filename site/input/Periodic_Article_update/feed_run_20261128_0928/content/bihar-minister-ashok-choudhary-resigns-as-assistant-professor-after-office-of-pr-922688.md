# Bihar minister Ashok Choudhary resigns as assistant professor after 'office of profit' charge

**Patna** — Bihar's food and consumer protection department minister Ashok Choudhary has submitted a resignation from an assistant professor position in the political science department at Patna's AN College, a senior official said on Friday. The move came after the minister faced backlash over his association with the "office of profit", which escalated to the Lok Bhavan, which referred it to the Advocate General.

Patliputra University (PPU) registrar Professor Abu Bakar confirmed the development, but did not mention the date from when the resignation would be effective. "After being selected by the Bihar State University Service Commission, I joined and applied for leave immediately. However, there has been no decision on my leave application due to which I have decided to resign," a university official quoted the resignation letter, adding that it would be placed before the University Syndicate.

Choudhary has been an MLC since March 17, 2021. He was appointed to the post in 2026. His term in the legislative council is due to end on March 16, 2027. He said on Friday that his case was not isolated. "Many legislators have been appointed assistant professor with me as well as before me and the law is one for all. I was appointed after full scrutiny and through a prescribed process and my appointment was also not hidden from anyone. If some people don't want me to continue as assistant professor, there is no point continuing," he said.

The move came after reports that the Advocate General has sought a discussion with the secretary and officer on special duty (judicial) at Lok Bhavan and the Vice Chancellor of PPU, which, an official at the AG's office said, is yet to take place.
