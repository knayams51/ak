# RJD MP seeks probe on embankment breaches

**Patna** — Buxar MP Sudhakar Singh has written to chief minister Samrat Choudhary, demanding an independent and timebound investigation into widespread embankment breach across several districts causing severe floods in Bihar and review of expenditure incurred on construction and maintenance of riverbanks and action taken against responsible officers and engineers.

"The repeated breach of embankments built to provide flood protection raises serious questions about the quality of their construction, maintenance, monitoring process and the accountability of the department concerned. This has made the flood situation grim in the state, affecting lakhs of people," he has written, urging the state government to seek special package in view of huge flood devastation.
