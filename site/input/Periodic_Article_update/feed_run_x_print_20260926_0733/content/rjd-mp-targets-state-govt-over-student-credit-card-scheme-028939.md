# RJD MP targets state govt over student credit card scheme ©

**Patna** — Buxar MP and senior RJD leader Sudhakar Singh on Friday said that the Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme, launched with the aim of helping students from economically weak and middle-class families in the state acquire higher education, has derailed from its original objective.

Demanding a white paper on the student credit card scheme, he demanded that the government immediately release old students' pending payment up to June, disburse funds to the new students and fix accountability on officials for delays beyond 30 days. "The government launched this scheme with a promise that no student from Bihar will be deprived of higher education due to economic hardship, but the district-wise data currently available is depicting a serious picture of its implementation. The number of students applying for financial incentive is large, but the disposal of their application, the sanctioning of the loan and the actual payment of the sanctioned amount is abysmal due to huge pendency at every stage," he added.
