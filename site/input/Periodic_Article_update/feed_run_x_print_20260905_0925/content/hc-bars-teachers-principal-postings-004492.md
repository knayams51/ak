# HC bars teachers’ principal postings

**Patna** — The Patna High Court has set aside the orders of the universities to send college teachers to the newly-constituted degree colleges on deputation without their consent.

"The order of May 27 and letter of June 4 are unsustainable in the absence of their due consent. The order of the vice chancellor passed on the representations of the petitioners negating their claim is also held to be unsustainable and accordingly set aside," said the order of Justice Harish Kumar on Tuesday. while hearing the petition of JP University (Chapra) teachers.

The matter related to deputation of college teachers to the newly-created 211 government degree colleges as principals in-charge.

The government declared these colleges as constituent units of the universities under whose jurisdiction they fell, but the court did not accept that due to absence of due procedures.

"Upon setting aside the impugned orders, any consequential order passed against the petitioners depriving them to put their signatures and withholding of salary also stood quashed and cancelled" the court observed.
