# JD(U) launches ‘Nitish Samvad’ on water treaty

**Patna** — With the Farakka Water Treaty, signed between India and Bangladesh in December 1996 and set to expire in December 2026, the Janata Dal (United), which is aggressively campaigning for the treaty's revamp (if renewed) to include Bihar's increased concerns in its ambit, has decided to launch a Nitish Samvad (Nitish Discourse), a drive to highlight the state's water concerns,

Party's working president Sanjay Jha, a Rajya Sabha MP, raised the issue in Parliament during the Monsoon Session and External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar wrote to him that New Delhi would keep Bihar's interests in mind during negotiations over the treaty.

The JD(U) has already started using clips of former Bihar CM and Rajya Sabha member Nitish Kumar's concems about the Farakka ordeal for Bihar.

"Any new treaty must keep Bihar's water needs up to 2050 in mind," said Jha.
