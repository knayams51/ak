# JD(U) upset as rebel MLCs ‘switched’ to BJP, calls review meeting on Nov 5

**Patna** — After Opposition parties seeking disqualification of the two newly-elected independent members of the Legislative Council -- Banshidhar Brajwasi and Rahul Raj -- for their reported induction into the BJP, NDA partner JD(U) has questioned the reported move and called for upholding of the "alliance dharma".

The party will have its Council poll performance review meeting on November 5 and the row over these two independents have made it an anticipated event.

The JD(U) is peeved as both the independents Banshidhar Brajwasi (Tirhut graduates' constituency) and Rahul Raj (Saran teachers' constituency) emerged victorious against JD(U) candidates Abhishek Kumar Jha and Anand Pushkar.

Raj was expelled from the BJP for six years for filing his nomination despite the seat going to the JD(U) in alliance.
