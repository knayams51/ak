**Patna** — Chief minister Samrat Chaudhary on Monday discussed in detail the strengthening of the infrastructure of national highways and expressways in Bihar and early start of pending projects during his meeting with Union minister for road transport and highways Nitin Gadkari at the latter's residence in New Delhi.

Expressing gratitude to Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Gadkari for their continued support for the development of roads and infrastructure in Bihar, the CM placed his request for early approval of major road projects to give major boost to connectivity in the state.

The CM informed the Union minister that four important projects in Bihar with a total length of about 330 km at an estimated cost of 249,751 crore have been approved by the PPPAC (Public Private Partnership Assessment Committee) of the Government of India.

"These projects are very important in terms of reducing the logistics cost and facilitating smooth traffic in the state, They are currently under consideration for final approval of the CCEA (Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs)." he added.

The projects include construction of NHO-727AA (2-lane bridge over Gandak river ata cost of 1976.77 crore), construc ion of bridge and access road connecting Manuapul (Betiah) in Bihar with Tiwaripatti (Sevrahi) in Uttar Pradesh, construction of 6 lane elevated and service road in Saristabad-Didarganj and Anisabad-Didarganj section in hybrid annuity mode (HAM mode) ata cost of 28,455.86 crore. construction of Patna-Purnia 4-lane Greenfield Expressway (NO-09, under HAM mode) at a cost of 231,987.11 crore and construc: tion of Varanasi-Ranchi-Kolkata 6 lane greenfield highway (from Konki village to Lerua village on HAM mode) at a cost of 28,455.40 crore.

During the meeting. the CM also urged the Union minister to notify the Bihar section of the Gorakhpur-Siliguni Expressway as the National Expressway.

"The state government is giving full support to NHAI in preconstruction work and the work of land acquisition (3A notification) in the concerned districts is in advance stage.
