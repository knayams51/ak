# J D(U) leaders tour UP, party eyes 2027 polls as NDA ally

**Patna** — Bihar health minister Nishant Kumar said on Sunday that his party was exploring the possibility of fighting upcoming assembly elections in Uttar Pradesh as an ally of the BJP-led NDA. Nishant, the son of JD(U) supremo Nitish Kumar, was in Varanasi with party leaders like Sanjay Jha, Shrawan Kumar, Jama Khan and others.

The JD(U) has been contesting polls in UP and it won two seats in 2002 and one in 2006.

"At present, there have been no formal talks with the BJP, but our senior leaders are discussing the matter and a decision will soon be taken. My effort is to take my father, former Bihar CM Nitish Kumar's development mindset forward and create a strong platform for the party," he told media persons in Varanasi.
