# JD(U) ‘feud’ continues: Neeraj licks wounds after poll victory

**Patna** — The intra-party feud in the Janata Dal (United) that plagued the party's legislative council poll campaign continues unbated even after the results as Neeraj Kumar, the party's sole winner, is not ready to forgive or forget colleagues who tried to mar his election.
