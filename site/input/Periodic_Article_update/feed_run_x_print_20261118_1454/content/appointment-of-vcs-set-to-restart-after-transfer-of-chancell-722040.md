# Appointment of VCs set to restart after transfer of Chancellor’s secy

**Patna** — The process for the appointment of vice-chancellors in Bihar's state universities is set to commence again after a brief lull, when there was sudden transfer of Dipak Kumar Singh, additional chief secretary to the Governor, after the completion of interaction of just two universities -- Veer Kunwar Singh University (Ara) and Tilka Manjhi Bhagalpur University.

The interaction for appointment of VCs in two universities was completed on November 3-4, which led to a lot of backlash in the social media over omission of certain names in the screening despite the fact that they were found eligible on previous occasions. Many of the aspiring candidates also petitioned the Lok Bhawan seeking to know the reason behind omission of their names.

Earlier this year, the interaction for two universities was cancelled and readvertisement was for them. As a result, the next phase got stalled.

Lok Bhawan attributed the omission of names in the screening to the move to make available details of vigilance or administrative proceedings against the candidates for the VC post to the selection committee, as many of the candidates were found to be facing some charges. Many other VCs facing charges are also in the pipeline for other universities.

He said that out of around 8,000 applications received for the post of VC in 10 state universities, there were many duplications, with one candidate applying for 7-8 universities and ifa candidate got rejected for one due to charges, he or she could get rejected for all others.

"If the Governor himself ordered vigilance probe against anyone, as it happened in case ofa former Magadh University VC, how could the same candidate's name be sent for vigilance clearance again. In some cases, clearance was sought from Lok Bhawan of another state and the file was returned without any comment. In that case the candidates got the benefit of doubt. We cannot allow the scenario of assistant professors' appointment to recur by ignoring verification," he added.
