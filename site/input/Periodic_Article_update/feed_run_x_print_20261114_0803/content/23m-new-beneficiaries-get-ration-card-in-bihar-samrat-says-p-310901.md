# 2.3m new beneficiaries get ration card in Bihar; Samrat says poor main govt focus

**Patna** — Chief minister Samrat Choudhary on Thursday distributed ration cards to over 2.37 million new beneficiaries, taking the total to 20.9 million ration cards, benefitting about 84.6 million people. He also distributed permits for 1.448 new public distribution system shops.

Addressing a function at Gyan Bhawan in Patna, Choudhary reiterated the government resolve that no poor of Bihar should go hungry and no eligible family should be deprived of the benefits of government schemes.

"Bringing happiness to the lives of the poor and providing them opportunities to live with dignity is the top priority of the government. That is how the dream of developed India of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and prosperous Bihar of former chief minister Nitish Kumar will be realised. Food security to poor and needy families is part of the larger objective," he said.
