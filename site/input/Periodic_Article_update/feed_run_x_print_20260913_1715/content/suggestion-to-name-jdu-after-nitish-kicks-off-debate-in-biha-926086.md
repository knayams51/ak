# Suggestion to name JD(U) after Nitish kicks off debate in Bihar

**Patna** — Janata Dal (United) working president and Rajya Sabha member Sanjay Jha's statement that the party should be named after former Bihar chief minister Nitish Kumar has triggered a debate in Bihar's volatile politics about the timing of his remark.

Jha's statement to name the party Janata Dal (Nitish) came at a time when there is another demand to change Bakhtiarpur's name to Nitishpur. Bihar minister and HAM-S leader Santosh Kumar Suman had raised the demand.

Earlier this year, Pataliputra University (PPU) Senate had approved the proposal for naming of the new campus of the university in Bakhtiarpur as "Nitish Nagar". Bakhtiarpur is the birthplace of Nitish Kumar.

Jha said that he first said about naming the party after Nitish Kumar at Begusarai and then at Khagaria as part of the ongoing "Nitish Samvad" programme to create awareness regarding the ill-effects of Farakka (Ganga) Water Treaty, which is set to expire in December, and the protection of Bihar's water rights.
