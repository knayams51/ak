# RJD MP seeks probe into medical purchase pricing, fund oversight

**Patna** — Rashtriya Janata Dal (RJD) MP Sudhakar Singh on Sunday raised concerns over the transparency behind government medical purchase procedures in Bihar, and questioning how supply prices are fixed for state hospitals and demanding stricter oversight on the use of public funds.

"Why there was a huge difference between the market rate and the government purchase rate and who is responsible for it?" he asked while talking to media persons on the alleged irregularities in the purchase by the Bihar Medical Services and Infrastructure Corporation Limited (BMSICL). Taking a note of the allegations that biodegradable bags and bins for microwave were purchased at several times the market rate, health minister Nishant Kumar had earlier assured that the matter will be thoroughly investigated..
