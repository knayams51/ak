# NDA pledges to show unity at Modi’s Nov 21 Bihar event

**Patna** — All constituents of the ruling National Democratic Alliance (NDA) in Bihar on Tuesday pledged to put up a united and full-fledged show at the upcoming event in Vaishali to be attended Prime Minister Narendra Modi on November 21. The NDA leaders met at the BJP headquarters in Patna.

The PM will not only inaugurate a development project but he will also lead a rally and the NDA leaders are bracing to make it a show of unity anda grand success.

The occasion marks the first anniversary of the new NDA government in Bihar. The government has smoothly seen the transition from chief ministership from Nitish Kumar to Samrat Choudhary.

Choudhary is the first BJP

CM of the state and has been at the helm for about six months.

At the meeting, BJP national general secretary and Bihar in-charge Harish Dwivedi, co-incharge Rohan Gupta, state party president Sanjay Saraogi, state president of LJP (Ram Vilas) Surendra Vivek, state president of HAM Party Dr Anil Kumar, RLM working state president Subhash Chandravanshi, Bihar ministers Shravan Kumar and Shri Ram Kripal Yadav and senior leaders and workers of the NDA were present.

"The meeting was to make the PM's visit a historic one and all the constituents of the NDA are committed for that. There will be a series of meetings as the build up for the grand show," said Saraogi.

JD(U) MLC and party spokesman Neeraj Kumar said that the NDA was one and there was no

distrust. "Some people are doing kite flying and they can be happy with that. The PM is coming to the cradle of democracy, Vaishali, on completion of one year of the NDA government and that in itself will be a message to those who point fingers at Constitution and Indian democracy," he added.

Political analysts see in PM's Bihar visit an opportunity to cement the ties among the constituents to send a strong message to the Opposition.

"There was some distrust among the NDA partners after the MLC polls and it also became apparent in more ways than one. It also created room for speculations. The results were also below par for both the parties -- JD(U) and BJP. With PM's visit, there is an opportunity for the alliance to present a united show," said social analyst NK Choudhary.
