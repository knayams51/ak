# CM reiterates pay-scale pledge for teachers ahead of MLC polls

**Patna** — 'arants @hiedustancimes com

Setting the tone for the Bihar Legislative Council elections for eight seats - four each from the graduate and teachers' constituencies - scheduled in November, Bihar chief minister Samrat Choudhary on Sunday reiterated his government's resolve to give pay-scale to the teachers of unaided institutions.

He was speaking a day after releasing 2461-crore grant-inaid for payment of long pending salary to teachers and employces of unaided institutions ata function to felicitate him for his announcement of pay-scale.

Bihar Vidhan Parishad chairman Avadhesh Narayan Singh and several MLCs from teacher and graduate constituencies were present at the function having a large presence of teachers from unaided institutions.

"Right now the government has released 246l-crore. but a lot more is in the pipeline. It is to the tune of %,700-crore. There is no dearth of money. 1 will meet you again and do full justice. It is the government's responsibility. You just have to keep showering your blessings," headded

The CM said that all the MLCS seated in the hall championed the cause of teachers of unaided institutions.

"You all will certainly get the pay-scale. There are certain issues which are being resolved. I will hold discussions with you all the stakeholders.
