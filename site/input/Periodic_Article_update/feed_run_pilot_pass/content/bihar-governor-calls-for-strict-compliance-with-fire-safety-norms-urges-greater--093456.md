# Bihar Governor calls for strict compliance with fire safety norms, urges greater awareness | Hindustan Times

**Patna** — Governor Lt Gen Syed Ata Hasnain (Retd) on Friday discussed various issues related to fire safety audit, its compliance and fire safety in hotels and hospitals in Bihar with Bihar State Disaster Management Authority (BSDMA) member PN Rai, Bihar Fire Services IG M Sunil Naik and DIG Sudhir Kumar Porika.

The Governor, who was earlier member of the National Disaster a management Authority (NDMA), directed to organise a seminar at Bihar Lok Bhawan with the aim of increasing public awareness towards fire safety.

"Representatives of NDMA and BSDMA, functionaries of Health Department and Bihar Fire Service, operators of hospitals and hotels in various cities of the state and their security officials, representatives of urban local bodies and other concerned stakeholders will participate in this seminar," he added.

Having extensive experience in various aspects of disaster management, risk mitigation, pre-disaster preparedness and emergency response mechanism, the Governor's guidance is expected to help towards better compliance with safety standards, expansion of public awareness and adoption of best disaster management practises in the state, which is in sport light after big disasters in Muzaffarpur and elsewhere.

In the meeting, the Governor was informed that the process of fire safety audit and necessary compliance related to it has been completed in 1,679 hospitals out of a total of 1,943 hospitals in Bihar and 3,744 hotels out of a total of 4,020 hotels.

"The remaining hospitals and hotels have been directed to ensure compliance with fire safety standards and other provisions laid down by the government at the earliest. They have been clearly warned that in case of non-compliance with the prescribed standards, action will be taken against them as per the rules," the Governor was told.
