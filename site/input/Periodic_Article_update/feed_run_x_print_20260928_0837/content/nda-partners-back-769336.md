# NDA partners back

**Patna** — The Election Commission of India (ECI)'s statement denying allegations of an internal rift and clarifying its stand on the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls seems to have put an end to differing tones within the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) in Bihar. Union minister and LJP-RV chief Chirag Paswan, who had asked EC to "clear its stand in the wake of misgivings in the minds of the people", said that the election body's pointed reply would "further strengthens public trust in democracy and expose the Opposition game-plan".

"Whenever any question or concern arises in the public's mind, it is my responsibility to bring it forward and ensure a clear resolution. Our expectation was that the EC would take those concerns seriously and clarify every aspect and it has happened. The process of SIR, the unanimous agreement of all three commissioners, and the facts related to the meeting with the Cabinet Secretary have been clearly presented in the EC reply. This will strengthen public trust in democracy," he added, in his post on X.

Bihar BJP president Sanjay Sarogi said that the Congress and the opposition like the RJD were only engaged in the politics of spreading confusion, as they had lost complete trust of the people. "Wherever they win, everything is fine; and wherever they lose, they point fingers at constitutional institutions by making excuses like EVM, SIR or something else," he added.
